Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-06-14  ·  Directive Craft LLC

This website does not track you. No analytics. No cookies. No advertising. No data collection of any kind. If you contact us by email, we receive your email — that’s it. There is nothing else to disclose.

CookiesNone
AnalyticsNone
Tracking scriptsNone
AdvertisingNone
Data soldNever
Account requiredNo

Information we collect

The directivecraft.com website does not collect, store, or process any personal information about visitors. There are no analytics tools, tracking pixels, session recording scripts, or advertising networks on this site.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard server-side request data (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) as part of normal hosting infrastructure. This data is subject to GitHub’s Privacy Statement and is not accessible to or controlled by Directive Craft LLC.

Email contact

If you contact us by email, we receive and read your message. We use that information only to respond to you. We do not add you to mailing lists, share your address with third parties, or use your contact for any purpose beyond the conversation you initiated.

Our email uses privacy-focused forwarding from our domain address to a private inbox. No message content is stored on intermediate servers beyond standard delivery processing.

Fonts and external resources

All fonts on this site are self-hosted from directivecraft.com. This site makes no requests to any third-party server — no font CDN, no script host, no external stylesheet, no image host. Nothing is loaded from a domain other than this one.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set cookies. It does not use localStorage, sessionStorage, or any other client-side storage mechanism.

Third-party services

This site does not integrate with social media platforms, advertising networks, payment processors, or any other third-party services. No share buttons, like buttons, or embedded third-party content is present.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a revised effective date. Because no data is collected, substantive changes are unlikely.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address on our contact page.
